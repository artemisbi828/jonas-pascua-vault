The command index is `actor-command.md`. It's the registry list of commands (slash prompts, skills and the like), and the `scope` column says whether each one is global or domain-specific. This is the list the global preferences refer to.

Two related places:

- **Command sources:** the files that define the commands are listed in `ai-preferences config.json`, under `files`, `extra_prompts` and `prompt_skill_mirrors`. `/provision-local` comes from `provision-local.prompt.md`.
- **Tool index:** `toolkit-product-index.md` lists the tool projects (like provision-local) rather than the commands.
