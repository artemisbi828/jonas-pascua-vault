- **Cognitive friction** ← **best overall**
   
    - Not necessarily difficult.
    - It makes the person do a little extra interpreting, translating, remembering, or navigating.
    - One occurrence is cheap; repeated occurrences accumulate.

- **Cognitive tax** ← best when emphasizing accumulation
    
    - A small recurring cost imposed on the user.
    - Especially good for unnecessary work.
    - “Each ambiguous label imposes a small cognitive tax.”

- **Cognitive overhead** ← best technical/architectural term
    
    - Mental work required **around** the actual task rather than by the task itself.
    - Nice parallel to computational overhead.

- **Cognitive drag**
    
    - Emphasizes the cumulative slowing effect.
    - Less precise, but punchy: “Individually trivial, collectively it creates cognitive drag.”
